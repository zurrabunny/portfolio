<h3>What this is all about :0</h3>

<pblog>
Tracks like "Flower Basket", "Starstruck", "Shorts and Shores", and "Unholy Seranata" are actually remixed versions of
the old songs I made back in 2021–2022! I wanted to include them here because they were part of my music journey. We
can’t forget the tracks that started everything <:0<br><br>

<strong>Please note that some of these have BAD MIXING !!!</strong> The volume slider should help you avoid that pain if necessary :')<br><br>

I will also be pointing out some flaws of the old versions, and how I improved on it in the current versions!
</pblog>

<h3>Flower Basket (2021)</h3>

<pblog>
Flower Basket is the 2nd out of the 4 songs I made for the first week of my old Friday Night Funkin' mod, "Friday Night Honeymoon"<br>
The problem in the original version is that its incredibly repetitive with no variance on the melody / chords. Paired with
bad mixing (lack of mixing even), it really discourages me from listening to this track, even back then. it ended up being
a filler song for a good while.<br><br>

The new version made me think of mixing ahead of time, and with the help of OnlineBlanks, we made a livelier, upbeat
rendition of Flower Basket >:D
</pblog>

<h3>Starstruck (2021)</h3>

<pblog>
Starstruck is the 3rd out of the 4 songs I made for the first week of "Friday Night Honeymoon"<br>
The problem in the original version is that it sounds very bold for a calm, space-esque song, especially the bass and the
multitude of percussion that got mixed in the song, the bitcrush also messes with the mixing a lot. The arp is also very distracting<br><br>

I got rid of the heavy bass and got rid of all of the percussion, and added a healthy amount of reverb in the mixing, as well as
reworking the arp. I also made the song "wider" to make it feel as if youre actually floating in space. <:D
</pblog>

<h3>Take A Minute (2021) / Old Shorts and Shores</h3>

<pblog>
Take A Minute is the final song of the 4 songs I made for the first week of "Friday Night Honeymoon"<br>
Theres not really much problems here besides the mixing, some percussions still pierce my ears. Despite The first week taking
place on a beach, the main problem of this song is that it doesnt feel "beachy" enough.

I listened to a lot of beach tracks in soundcloud when I was in the process of remaking this, the one thing they all share in
common is that they have percussion akin to slapping your hand on some random object in the beach, a rhodes instrument, a guitar,
a synth pluck, and sounds from the beach itself. i took those ideas and added it in the current version of Take A Minute, Shorts and Shores! :D
</pblog>

<h3>Fallen Star (2022) / Old Unholy Seranata</h3>

<pblog>
Fallen Star is the final song of the 3 songs I made for the second week of "Friday Night Honeymoon"<br>

By this point, id say that i was good enough at mixing to not make most music elements pierce my ears, theres not much problems here
other than the song feeling too empty / stale. This song is about Aurora, a self-loathing angel, having a break down. Me and OnlineBlanks felt that
we can match the "breakdown" vibe better, and we did! I'm still really proud of what we made, I would consider it the best track we've ever made >:D
</pblog>
