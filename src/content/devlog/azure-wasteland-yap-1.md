<h4>"one can find beauty even in a world full of chaos"</h4>

<pblog>
The world of Azure Wasteland is a lush interpretation of what remains after a near-extinction event. New civilizations, new flora and fauna, new geographical wonders, and forgotten wastelands.
The land is warped, pushing familiar elements to their extremes, alongside creatures shaped by their environments.
<br><br>
It is a fresh take on a world formed by a blend of familiar cultures and cultures beyond our understanding, where everyday rituals exist beside practices that feel alien and unknowable.
A story of familiar goods and evils, driven by intentions that closely reflect our everyday wills and urges. Familiar strife and struggles, all seen through a different pen and a different lens.
