    <h3 class="char-description-title">Character Description</h3>
    <div class="char-description-line"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="info-stuffs">
  Aurora Valenne is a divine being known as the Bearer of Knowledge.
  She walks among mortals in secret, often pretending to be “just another being,”
  while secretly carrying the weight of her divinity. She tends to
  hide in wooded areas, usually alone. She cannot die by normal means,
  making her nearly immortal, but in return, she suffers the consequences
  of her own flaws. Though she desires freedom from her responsibilities,
  her divine nature always catches up with her, forcing her to confront her
  imperfections.
  </div>

    <h3 class="char-description-title">Appearance</h3>
    <div class="char-description-line"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="info-stuffs">
She has long teal hair and bluish-light gray skin, with angel wings that shed
feathers whenever she feels threatened. Her hair clip takes the form of the Fortitude
Angelic Symbol. She wears a long green cloth laced with gold, gold-encrusted slippers,
and gold hand plates. A small wing decoration is attached to her shoulder with a gold
holder. Usually, only one of her eyes is visible, as her bangs partially conceal the other.
  </div>

    <div class="char-description-line"></div>
  </div>

  <div class="info-stuffs">
  Aurora is a divine being tormented by self-loathing. She views sin as inherent
  to herself and struggles with both contempt for the flaws of mortals and her own
  imperfections. She is introspective, critical, and burdened by her responsibilities,
  yet yearns to experience freedom among humans.
