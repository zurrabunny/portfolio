  <div class="info-stuffs">
  Agatha Honeydew is the caring mother of the Honeydew family and the wife of Endicore Honeydew.
  She lives in the serene neighborhood of Azalea Hills and works as a flight attendant for Horizon Airlines.
  Due to her job, she is often away from home, returning only about 18 days per month. The airline she works
  for typically sends two-thirds of her earnings to Endicore, who is usually at home more often than she is.
