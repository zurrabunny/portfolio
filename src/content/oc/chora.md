  Despite this, she has mastered the art of appearing calm, confident, and knowledgeable

  <div class="info-stuffs">
  <ul>
  <li>The End Dimension operates on a strict caste system: outer rings closer to the center are dangerous and infested with Ender Dragons, while farther rings like Chora’s are safer but highly competitive.
  <li>Each ring contains clusters of islands, calculated as 40 × ring number × 2. Chora’s cluster contains 1,600 islands.
  <li>The higher a cluster’s number, the richer it is economically. Clusters can swap places based on wealth.
  <li>The primary currency is Spire Armor Trims, while diamonds are tightly regulated to prevent inflation.
  <li>Each cluster must have 80% of inhabitants earn a minimum of 2000 × nth outer ring Spire Trims per ring year. For Chora, that’s 40,000 Spire Trims per year.
